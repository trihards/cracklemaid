Listening with the screen off

Start the fire, then lock your phone. On recent iPhones (iOS 17.5+) the live fire usually keeps playing, and lock-screen controls can pause it. Browsers still decide when background audio stops, so if your fire goes quiet after locking:

  • switch Playback to Steady loop,
  • try it in a Safari tab as well as from the Home Screen icon,
  • keep other audio apps from taking over.

The sleep timer's fade runs on the audio clock, so it still fades even if the page itself is asleep.

Every crackle is synthesized live, with no recordings and no loops (unless you ask for one). Cracklemaid is a mermaid, not a fire hazard. 🧜🏽‍♀️
